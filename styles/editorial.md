# Editorial

Use this register for an independent retailer, maker, bookshop, florist, or product-led local shop. It must feel selected, current, and rooted in a point of view.

## Conversion priority

Show the strongest product or seasonal story first. Keep directions, opening hours, and the main visit or shop action easy to reach. Let the photographs create desire, then use precise copy to explain why the collection belongs here.

## Typography and spacing

Pair an expressive serif with a quiet sans-serif. Use very large headlines, tight leading, small uppercase utility text, and visible editorial rules. Mix broad areas of space with compact caption groups. Do not use a uniform card grid.

## Section rhythm

Treat the page as a short independent magazine. Open with a cover-like hero. Move into an asymmetric contact sheet, a concise shop story, selected offers, and practical visit details. A section can change scale sharply, but the type and rules must hold the sequence together.

## Imagery

Use products in context, shelves, packaging, hands, and the street outside. Mix one commanding image with smaller details. Crop with intent. Keep numbered captions short. Avoid generic lifestyle images that could belong to any shop.

## Palette

Use warm paper, dark ink, and one print-like accent. Derive the accent from a product label, sign, or logo. Keep most of the page quiet so merchandise color stays dominant.

## Keep

Expressive serif titles, quiet sans-serif details, print rules, square edges, and asymmetric image groups.

The preset is a design direction, not a fixed layout. Keep its visual register recognizable.

## Vary

Use the strongest collection or seasonal product to choose the hero composition. Let the product range control image scale and content density.

Derive these changes from the shop brand, photographs, content, and customer actions:

- Hero composition.
- Section order and emphasis.
- Image proportions and cropping.
- Content density.
- Type scale within the preset register.
- Spacing rhythm.
- Borders, dividers, shapes, and image treatment within the preset register.
- Restrained CSS motion and interaction effects.

Put shop-specific CSS in `src/styles/site.css`. The agent can edit Astro components when business evidence requires a different composition.

Require business evidence for each decorative choice. Do not use generic gradients, random blobs, excessive animation, or unrelated visual effects. Preserve reduced-motion behavior.

Two sites with this preset must share a register, but must not look interchangeable. Their brands, images, content, and customer actions must shape the result.
