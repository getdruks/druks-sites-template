# Sharp

Use this register for a barber, tattoo studio, repair craft, or direct service with a strong local identity. It must feel skilled, immediate, and unmistakable.

## Conversion priority

Put booking, call, or walk-in status first. Show the core services and price ranges without ceremony. Add real work, the barber or maker, the chair or bench, and the exact location. Make appointment and walk-in rules explicit.

## Typography and spacing

Use a heavy condensed display face with a plain sans-serif for details. Use uppercase headings, hard edges, thick rules, and tight blocks. Scale must be bold, but body text must remain calm and readable. Do not imitate nightclub or gaming graphics.

## Section rhythm

Start with a poster-like hero. Stack service rows like a price board. Use the gallery as evidence, not decoration. Keep credentials concise. Use strong section boundaries and little visual ambiguity. The page can feel dense, but the booking path must stay obvious.

## Imagery

Use honest portraits, finished work, close tool details, and the shop interior. High contrast is useful. Preserve skin and material detail. Avoid generic beards, dark stock portraits, skull motifs, and heritage props that do not belong to the real shop.

## Palette

Use near-black, a light workwear neutral, and one forceful accent. Derive the accent from signage, a chair, an apron, or the logo. Keep it on actions, markers, and short labels.

## Keep

Heavy condensed uppercase titles, hard edges, strong rules, direct service rows, and a visible booking path.

The preset is a design direction, not a fixed layout. Keep its visual register recognizable.

## Vary

Use the sign, workbench, chair, or finished work to choose the hero composition. Let the actual service range control density and price-board emphasis.

Derive these changes from the shop brand, photographs, content, and customer actions:

- Hero composition.
- Section order and emphasis.
- Image proportions and cropping.
- Content density.
- Type scale within the preset register.
- Spacing rhythm.
- Borders, dividers, shapes, and image treatment within the preset register.
- Restrained CSS motion and interaction effects.

Put shop-specific CSS in `src/styles/site.css`. The agent can edit Astro components when business evidence requires a different composition.

Require business evidence for each decorative choice. Do not use generic gradients, random blobs, excessive animation, or unrelated visual effects. Preserve reduced-motion behavior.

Two sites with this preset must share a register, but must not look interchangeable. Their brands, images, content, and customer actions must shape the result.
