# Clear

Use this register for a plumber, repair service, or practical local business. It must feel fast, capable, and easy to trust.

## Conversion priority

Put the call or quote action in the first view. State the service area beside it. Keep the open status, response time, license, insurance, and guarantee close to the action. Do not make a visitor search for basic proof.

## Typography and spacing

Use one direct sans-serif family. Use strong weight changes instead of decorative type. Keep headings compact and readable. Use a regular spacing rhythm. Keep the page dense enough for urgent scanning, but never cramped.

## Section rhythm

Start with a clear offer and contact action. Place credentials and services where the customer needs them. Use thin rules to divide facts. Keep service names, scope, and price or response time on one visual line when space permits. The location block must state the exact service area.

## Imagery

Use real staff, vehicles, tools, and completed work. Favor clear daylight and useful context. Avoid stock handshakes, staged call-center photos, sliders, and abstract technology art.

## Palette

Start with a pale neutral surface, dark blue or charcoal text, and one high-contrast action color. Rebuild these colors from the shop logo and vehicle livery. Keep the action color reserved for links, status, and the primary action.

## Keep

Direct sans-serif type, compact headings, rectangular images, thin rules, and a clear contact path.

The preset is a design direction, not a fixed layout. Keep its visual register recognizable.

## Vary

Use a work result, service vehicle, or technician to choose the hero composition. Give urgent services more space than the shop story.

Derive these changes from the shop brand, photographs, content, and customer actions:

- Hero composition.
- Section order and emphasis.
- Image proportions and cropping.
- Content density.
- Type scale within the preset register.
- Spacing rhythm.
- Borders, dividers, shapes, and image treatment within the preset register.
- Restrained CSS motion and interaction effects.

Put shop-specific CSS in `src/styles/site.css`. The agent can edit Astro components when business evidence requires a different composition.

Require business evidence for each decorative choice. Do not use generic gradients, random blobs, excessive animation, or unrelated visual effects. Preserve reduced-motion behavior.

Two sites with this preset must share a register, but must not look interchangeable. Their brands, images, content, and customer actions must shape the result.
