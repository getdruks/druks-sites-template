# Vital

Use this register for a health coach, nutrition professional, trainer, therapist, or evidence-led wellness practice. It must feel calm, human, and qualified.

## Conversion priority

State who the service is for and what happens in the first session. Use booking as the main action. Put current credentials, method, privacy detail, and scope of practice near the top. Make health claims precise. Do not promise a guaranteed outcome.

## Typography and spacing

Pair a readable serif with a clear sans-serif. Use open line spacing, moderate title scale, soft corners, and long pauses between subjects. The page must lower cognitive load. Avoid pale text, tiny labels, and overlong centered copy.

## Section rhythm

Open with the practitioner or the real place of care. Bring credentials forward. Follow with a small set of offers, the process, proof, common questions, and low-pressure booking. Use rounded forms in media and trust blocks, but keep the layout disciplined.

## Imagery

Use the real practitioner, consultation space, movement, food, or equipment in natural light. Show people with dignity and specificity. Avoid supplement piles, medical cosplay, extreme transformation photos, and dark neon gym scenes.

## Palette

Use a light mineral or plant-toned surface, deep green or charcoal text, and one living accent. Derive all three from the practice identity and photographs. Keep the palette calm and maintain strong text contrast.

## Keep

Readable serif titles, clear body text, open leading, soft corners, and visible professional evidence.

The preset is a design direction, not a fixed layout. Keep its visual register recognizable.

## Vary

Use the practitioner, practice space, and first-session process to choose the hero composition. Let the service scope control trust placement and content density.

Derive these changes from the shop brand, photographs, content, and customer actions:

- Hero composition.
- Section order and emphasis.
- Image proportions and cropping.
- Content density.
- Type scale within the preset register.
- Spacing rhythm.
- Borders, dividers, shapes, and image treatment within the preset register.
- Restrained CSS motion and interaction effects.

Put shop-specific CSS in `src/styles/site.css`. The agent can edit Astro components when business evidence requires a different composition.

Require business evidence for each decorative choice. Do not use generic gradients, random blobs, excessive animation, or unrelated visual effects. Preserve reduced-motion behavior.

Two sites with this preset must share a register, but must not look interchangeable. Their brands, images, content, and customer actions must shape the result.
