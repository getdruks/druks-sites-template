# Warm

Use this register for a cafe, bakery, food counter, or welcoming neighborhood shop. It must feel appetizing, familiar, and easy to visit.

## Conversion priority

Show what is served, when the shop is open, and where it is. Keep directions, order, or table booking as the main action. Surface special hours and service changes early. A visitor on a phone must find the menu, hours, and directions in one short scan.

## Typography and spacing

Use a sturdy serif with a plain sans-serif. Let headings feel like a printed menu or shop notice. Use compact fact groups, comfortable body text, and small areas of warm space. Avoid decorative scripts and fake handwritten type.

## Section rhythm

Open with one appetite or atmosphere image. Move quickly into the current offer, gallery, and opening hours. Let the hours block feel like a menu board. Keep the story short. Finish with address, access detail, and the main action.

## Imagery

Use real dishes, drinks, ingredients, staff, counter details, and the street approach. Mix a table-wide scene with close food or craft details. Keep color natural. Avoid overhead stock spreads, empty cups, and photographs that hide portion or place.

## Palette

Use cream or warm paper, dark coffee or clay text, and one food or sign color. Derive the accent from the facade, crockery, packaging, or signature item. Keep red and orange accents small enough that the page does not feel like fast food.

## Keep

Sturdy menu-like serif titles, compact facts, warm surfaces, small rounded details, and easy access to hours and directions.

The preset is a design direction, not a fixed layout. Keep its visual register recognizable.

## Vary

Use the signature food, counter, or street approach to choose the hero composition. Let service hours and ordering behavior control the section emphasis.

Derive these changes from the shop brand, photographs, content, and customer actions:

- Hero composition.
- Section order and emphasis.
- Image proportions and cropping.
- Content density.
- Type scale within the preset register.
- Spacing rhythm.
- Borders, dividers, shapes, and image treatment within the preset register.
- Restrained CSS motion and interaction effects.

Put shop-specific CSS in `src/styles/site.css`. The agent can edit Astro components when business evidence requires a different composition.

Require business evidence for each decorative choice. Do not use generic gradients, random blobs, excessive animation, or unrelated visual effects. Preserve reduced-motion behavior.

Two sites with this preset must share a register, but must not look interchangeable. Their brands, images, content, and customer actions must shape the result.
