# Elegant

Use this register for a salon, beauty studio, spa, or high-touch appointment business. It must feel composed, personal, and exact.

## Conversion priority

Keep booking as the single main action. Show recent work, service scope, starting prices, appointment duration, and the people who provide the service. Put training, consultation requirements, and relevant safety notes before testimonials.

## Typography and spacing

Use a high-contrast serif for large titles and a restrained sans-serif for details. Use thin rules, long pauses, and generous margins. Keep navigation and buttons small, uppercase, and precise. Avoid soft script fonts and ornamental luxury symbols.

## Section rhythm

A tall portrait image is one useful hero option. Let each section hold one clear idea. Alternate spacious story passages with detailed service and policy lists. Keep the gallery irregular, like a careful portfolio. The booking section must close the page without a second competing action.

## Imagery

Use real work, real team members, clean tools, and a calm interior. Favor close details and honest skin or hair texture. Avoid stock models, excessive retouching, and empty images of candles or folded towels.

## Palette

Start with ivory, dark brown or black, and one muted metal or earth accent. Derive the accent from the logo, interior, or packaging. Keep contrast strong enough for small service and policy text.

## Keep

High-contrast serif titles, precise controls, thin rules, generous space, and a careful portfolio sequence.

The preset is a design direction, not a fixed layout. Keep its visual register recognizable.

## Vary

Use the actual work and consultation process to choose the hero composition. Vary portrait crops and service detail without losing the calm register.

Derive these changes from the shop brand, photographs, content, and customer actions:

- Hero composition.
- Section order and emphasis.
- Image proportions and cropping.
- Content density.
- Type scale within the preset register.
- Spacing rhythm.
- Borders, dividers, shapes, and image treatment within the preset register.
- Restrained CSS motion and interaction effects.

Put shop-specific CSS in `src/styles/site.css`. The agent can edit Astro components when business evidence requires a different composition.

Require business evidence for each decorative choice. Do not use generic gradients, random blobs, excessive animation, or unrelated visual effects. Preserve reduced-motion behavior.

Two sites with this preset must share a register, but must not look interchangeable. Their brands, images, content, and customer actions must shape the result.
